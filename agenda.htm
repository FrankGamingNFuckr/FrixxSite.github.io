Plan #1

Get rid of the Federal education system

Shift more education authority to states/local districts, reduce federal mandates, and focus on accountable, transparent standards.

Plan #2

Create a union/association for primary school parents and students

Build a structured way for parents/students to raise concerns, request transparency, and advocate for learning-focused classrooms.

Separate church and state

Keep government spaces and policies neutral on religion while protecting everyone’s right to personal beliefs and expression.
